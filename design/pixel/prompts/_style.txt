Art style: pixel art in the style of the game Eastward (Pixpil, 2021). Detailed, hand-placed pixel art with chunky, clearly visible square pixels on one strict grid (every art pixel is an ART_PX x ART_PX block of image pixels, all the same size), no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients (dithering only), crisp outlines in dark warm colours (never pure black), a limited warm nostalgic palette of about 32 colours: sun-bleached teal, rust orange, mossy green, cream, warm browns, soft violet shadows. Three-quarter top-down camera like Eastward and Stardew Valley, orthographic, no perspective, light from the upper left.
