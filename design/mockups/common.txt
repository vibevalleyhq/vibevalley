The game is "VibeValley 农场", a cozy Chinese farming web game in the spirit of the classic 2009-era social farm games (like the old QQ Farm), but with NO real brand logos or trademarks. Each farm plot is a coding task that grows through stages.

Characters (important):
- The helpers are cute little ANIMALS, all different species, each wearing a small farm outfit (straw hat, overalls or apron) and working with farm tools. They are: a bear (老周), a rabbit (小吴), a little fox (阿郑), a cat (Kimi), an owl (Grok), and a hedgehog (阿Pi). No human helpers.
- The farm manager is a friendly adult HUMAN farmer (straw hat, overalls). The player only talks to this person, who sends the animal helpers to the plots.

Everything below must be visible on the main screen:
- Farm: 6 diamond-shaped soil plots in an isometric grid, centered. Each plot shows a different state: (1) just seeded, with a small wooden tag; (2) a tiny sprout; (3) a leafy plant with the rabbit watering it; (4) a flowering plant with the fox kneeling to check it; (5) a ripe golden crop with sparkles and a floating bubble "可收获"; (6) a withered plant with small cartoon bugs and a red "!" bubble, the bear standing next to it scratching its head. One more plot is overgrown with weeds and a floating bubble "待批准". Two locked plots at the edge carry a little wooden sign "Lv.5 开垦".
- A farmhouse at the back. The human farm manager stands at the door with a speech bubble: "好，派阿郑去修字幕面板。" A small dog naps nearby.
- Background scenery: sky, distant hills, a wooden fence, a few trees.
- UI, with these Chinese labels:
  - top-left: round player avatar, "Lv.7", a green experience bar, the title "VibeValley 农场"
  - top-right: a gold coin counter "1,280" and a clock "10:40"
  - left side: a vertical column of round buttons labeled 商店, 仓库, 任务, 日志
  - bottom center: a tool bar of round tool icons labeled 手, 浇水, 杀虫, 除草, 收获, 铲除
  - bottom: a friends bar with 6 round portraits of the animal helpers, in this order with these names: bear 老周, rabbit 小吴, fox 阿郑, cat Kimi, owl Grok, hedgehog 阿Pi (the last name is exactly the two characters 阿 and Pi)
  - just above the tool bar: a chat bar with a microphone button and a text field with placeholder "对管家说…"
